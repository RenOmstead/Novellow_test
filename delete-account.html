Deleting your account

You can delete your Novellow account and everything in it whenever you like, from the Novellow app or the website. It takes a minute, and nobody will try to talk you out of it.

In the app or on the website

  1. Open the Novellow app, or go to novellow.com, and sign in.
  2. Open Settings from the menu.
  3. Under Privacy and your account, choose Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. If you'd like to keep a copy of your library first, choose Download all my data in Settings before you delete it.

If you can't sign in

Write to novellow.contact@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with "Please delete my Novellow account" as the subject. To keep your library safe from anyone pretending to be you, Novellow will check the request came from that address, then delete the account within 7 days and write back to say it's done.

What's deleted

Everything that belongs to your account, for good:

  • your sign-in (email address and password) and your profile: display name, username and bio;
  • your library: shelves, books, reading dates and progress, ratings, reviews, journal notes, quotes, words, questions, characters, themes, challenges, reading sessions and any cover pictures you uploaded;
  • your room: theme, furniture, decorations and colours;
  • Community: your friendships and friend requests, the book clubs and buddy reads you started (with everything posted in them), and everything you posted in other people's;
  • the notes you sent to Novellow, any replies, and your sign-in log.

What's kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept on purpose. Copies can stay in the backups and security logs of the services Novellow relies on for a short time, usually no more than 30 days, before they're overwritten. If the law requires something to be kept for longer, only that is kept, and only for as long as required. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Questions: novellow.contact@gmail.com.
