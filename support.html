Help and support

Novellow is a cozy illustrated library and reading journal, made with care by a reader. If something isn't working, or you have a question, you're very welcome to get in touch.

Getting in touch

Write to novellow.contact@gmail.com, or, once you're signed in, send a note from About Novellow → Report a problem or share an idea. Every message is read, and you'll usually hear back within two days.

Common questions

I didn't get the email to confirm my account

It comes from hello@novellow.com and can take a few minutes. Check your spam or junk folder. If it still isn't there, try signing in: Novellow will offer to send it again.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot your password? and enter your email address. Open the link in the email to choose a new one.

Someone is being unkind, or posted something that shouldn't be there

Every post in a book club or buddy read, every friend and every open library has a Report button. Reports go only to Novellow, are read within 24 hours, and the reader isn't told who sent it. You can also Block them: you won't see their posts, and they can't visit your library or send you friend requests. Unblock anyone in Settings.

Can I keep a copy of my library?

Yes. In Settings, choose Download my library or Download all my data.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings, choose Delete my account. It deletes everything straight away. Deleting your account has the details, and what to do if you can't sign in.

Does Novellow work without the internet?

Your library is kept safely online, so Novellow needs a connection to open it. Without one, it shows a page asking you to try again once you're back online.
